Three parts work well:

- Selecting a country moves the map to it and shows a useful identity summary.
- Four named modes and closed Appearance and Regions sections keep the start simple.
- An invalid cut gives a useful correction. A failed clipboard copy leaves the link selected for manual copying.

The priorities are:

1. **[P1] Explain and protect how users keep their work.** After a transfer and merge, opening Share left the editor at its original URL. Reloading returned the original map and disabled Undo. No warning appeared. A casual user can lose an experiment without understanding why. Show the session-only state after the first edit. Make “Copy a link to keep this map” explicit, and add protection against accidental reloads. Keep the account-free model. The relevant flow is in [App.tsx](/Users/alexwinter/WebProjects/altborder/src/App.tsx:2176). Suggested commands: `$impeccable harden` and `$impeccable clarify`.

2. **[P2] Keep the next mobile action visible.** At 390 × 844, the open region picker pushed Destination and Transfer out of view. The merge action and part of a cut error also fell below the visible panel. Scrolling still reached them. The fixed panel height and nested scrolling make the next step harder to find. Keep a compact selection summary and action area visible. Let the picker collapse when the user proceeds, and keep errors beside the action. See [the mobile capture](/Users/alexwinter/WebProjects/altborder/output/playwright/critique/a/mobile-transfer-region-selected.png) and [styles.css](/Users/alexwinter/WebProjects/altborder/src/styles.css:1961). Suggested command: `$impeccable adapt`.

3. **[P2] Show the full merge selection before applying it.** Adding Brazil and Bolivia through search left the map tightly zoomed on Acre. The list showed both countries, but their full area was not visible. This makes the result harder to check. Frame the combined selection, or add “Show selected countries” beside the count. See [the merge capture](/Users/alexwinter/WebProjects/altborder/output/playwright/critique/a/desktop-merge-ready.png) and [App.tsx](/Users/alexwinter/WebProjects/altborder/src/App.tsx:1932). Suggested command: `$impeccable shape`.

**Mental effort is low at the start and moderate in longer mobile flows.** The review marked three checklist failures: long choice groups, too many visible choices, and loss of context during scrolling. The expanded region picker showed five choices at once; Brazil has 27 regions. Search helps. The main problem is keeping the selection and next action together, not the number of regions in the map.

The start invites exploration, and a visible territory change gives a clear result. Searching for a mobile action can interrupt that progress. Reloading and losing work can undermine the ending. These emotional effects are design hypotheses.

The main concerns for different users are:

- **Jordan, a first-time user:** Share explains the recipient’s view, but not how Jordan keeps the experiment.
- **Casey, a distracted phone user:** selected items and the next action can be in different parts of the scrolling panel.
- **Riley, a user who tests limits:** reloading the edited root URL loses the changes; opening the generated share link restores them.
