# AltBorder logo options: round 2

The first three options were rejected. This round explores three new ideas:

- D · Faultline: custom lowercase lettering with a border cut.
- E · Contour: a flowing lowercase ab symbol.
- F · Own World: a flag with an invented border.

These are PNG concept boards made with the built-in ImageGen tool. The exact prompts are saved beside the images. They are proposals, not approved brand assets. They do not replace app files or change DESIGN.md.

Open `mockups.html` to compare the boards. The chosen design will need a final vector drawing and small-size checks before it is used in the app.
